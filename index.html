Why It Matters

Why Detail Your Car?

A regular wash handles surface dirt. Detailing goes further, cleaning the places a quick wash skips.

01

Protect the Paint

Dirt, grit, and road grime left on a car's surface can dull the finish over time. A thorough cleaning helps the paint last and keeps it looking its best.

02

Fight Winter Wear

Road salt and grime from Utah winters build up on wheels, trim, and the lower body. Getting it off regularly helps prevent damage that adds up over the years.

03

A Cleaner Cabin

Seats, carpets, and vents collect dust, crumbs, and pet hair. A deep interior clean makes the car nicer to be in, and easier to sell when the time comes.
